How to read it

  • Publication: a headline with source, date and link.
  • Candidate event: several publications grouped as possibly the same episode.
  • Official document: a document published by a public body; it states the body’s position.
  • Contrast: a published fact-check linked as a candidate match, never as an automatic verdict.
